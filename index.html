    A clearer way to wire.

    1. Start with a diagram. Upload a PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, or SVG, or explore the example. Uploading a picture does not recognize wires.
    2. Trace each connection. Choose Trace, tap points along a wire, then Finish wire. Give it a name and color. Hold Shift to align the next point.
    3. Make it precise. Choose Edit and select a wire. Drag its points; double-click a segment to add a point. Select a point and choose Remove point to delete it.
    4. Track the build. In Track mode, tap a wire or check it off in the list. At crossings, choose from the overlapping wires. Hidden wires can always be restored from the checklist.
    5. Keep a backup. Export includes the diagram, paths, display preferences, and progress. Import it on another device. Undo/redo history lasts for the current session.

    Zoom with the wheel or +/− controls. Pan with Pan mode or Space + drag. Touch: pinch to zoom and use two fingers to pan. Ctrl/Cmd Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd Shift Z redoes. Enter finishes a trace; Escape cancels it. The example is an illustrative practice layout, not a verified soldering plan.
